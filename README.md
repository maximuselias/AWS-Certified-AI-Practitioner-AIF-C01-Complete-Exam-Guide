## What Is AWS Certified AI Practitioner AIF-C01?

AWS Certified AI Practitioner is focused on foundational knowledge of AI and ML technologies and their applications on AWS.

The exam covers concepts such as:

- Artificial Intelligence fundamentals
- Machine Learning fundamentals
- Generative AI
- Foundation models
- Prompt engineering
- Responsible AI
- AI security
- Compliance and governance
- AWS AI and ML services

You do not need to be an advanced machine learning engineer to begin preparing for this certification. The exam is intended to test foundational knowledge rather than advanced model development skills.

## AIF-C01 Exam Domains

The AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam is divided into five major domains.

### 1. Fundamentals of AI and ML – 20%

This domain focuses on the basic principles of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

Important topics include:

- AI concepts
- Machine learning models
- Deep learning
- Neural networks
- Computer vision
- Natural Language Processing
- Training and inference
- Bias and fairness
- Large Language Models

Important AWS services include Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Transcribe, Amazon Translate, Amazon Comprehend, Amazon Lex, and Amazon Polly.

### 2. Fundamentals of Generative AI – 24%

Generative AI is an important part of the AIF-C01 exam.

Candidates should understand:

- Generative AI concepts
- Foundation models
- Transformer models
- Embeddings
- Tokens
- Model training
- Model deployment
- Generative AI use cases

AWS services to study include:

- Amazon Bedrock
- Amazon SageMaker JumpStart
- PartyRock
- Amazon Q

### 3. Applications of Foundation Models – 28%

This is the largest domain in the AIF-C01 exam.

Foundation models are pre-trained models that can be adapted for different applications and use cases.

A major concept to understand is **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**. RAG combines information retrieval with a generative AI model so that applications can use relevant external information when generating responses.

Important AWS services include:

- Amazon OpenSearch
- Amazon Aurora
- Amazon Neptune
- Amazon DocumentDB
- Amazon RDS

### 4. Guidelines for Responsible AI – 14%

Responsible AI focuses on building AI systems that are safe, fair, transparent, and trustworthy.

Key topics include:

- Bias
- Fairness
- Transparency
- Explainability
- Human oversight
- Model performance

Relevant AWS services include:

- Amazon Bedrock Guardrails
- Amazon SageMaker Clarify
- Amazon SageMaker Model Monitor
- Amazon Augmented AI (A2I)
- Amazon SageMaker Model Cards

### 5. Security, Compliance, and Governance – 14%

Security and governance are essential when deploying AI systems.

Candidates should understand:

- Identity and access management
- Data protection
- Encryption
- Compliance
- Governance
- Security monitoring

Important AWS services include:

- AWS IAM
- Amazon Macie
- AWS Config
- Amazon Inspector
- Amazon SageMaker Model Cards

# How to Prepare for AIF-C01

A structured preparation strategy can make the certification process much easier.

## 1. Understand the Exam Domains

Start by reviewing all five domains and their percentage weight. Give extra attention to **Applications of Foundation Models**, as it represents the largest portion of the exam.

## 2. Learn Important AWS Services

Do not only memorize service names. Understand what each service does and which type of AI or ML use case it supports.

For example:

- **Amazon SageMaker** – machine learning development and deployment
- **Amazon Bedrock** – foundation models and Generative AI applications
- **Amazon Comprehend** – text analysis
- **Amazon Transcribe** – speech-to-text
- **Amazon Polly** – text-to-speech
- **Amazon Translate** – language translation

## 3. Practice Exam Questions

Practice questions can help you become familiar with the exam format and identify areas that require additional study.

## 4. Gain Practical Knowledge

Hands-on experience with AWS AI services can make the concepts easier to understand. Explore AI and ML services and try to connect each service with its practical use case.
